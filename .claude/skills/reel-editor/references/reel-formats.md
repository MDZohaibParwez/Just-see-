### Cinematic breakdown of the same montage (1080×1080, 60 fps, no audio stream in that file)
1. Establish: trees/sky from a low angle.
2. Introduce her: medium shot from the car window.
3. Introduce him: side profile inside the car (window as frame-in-frame).
4. Beauty close-ups: hair movement, soft background.
5. Couple wide shots in fog.
6. Flower field: yellow flowers in the foreground for depth.
7. Walking: wide + medium shots.
8. Emotional close-ups, some low-angle.
9. His solo look: patterned jacket against green.
10. Landscape + interaction: over-the-shoulder.
11. Her portrait.
12. Calm wide ending on stone wall and mountains.

- **Pattern:** environment → her → him → couple → nature → close-ups → solo → interaction → landscape ending.
- **Grade controls to use:** exposure, contrast, highlights/shadows, temperature/tint, saturation, HSL (calm the greens), curves, vignette, light bloom.
- **Without audio,** do not claim beat timings. With music: mark the vocal entries and beats, then put cuts on phrases and key lyrics on matching visuals.
